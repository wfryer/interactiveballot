About
Welcome to the NON-PARTISAN project, InteractiveBallot.com! This is an educational project created by Wes Fryer, a middle school teacher in Charlotte, North Carolina. Last updated 8 Oct 2024.
Goals
  1. Encourage voters to visit OFFICIAL candidate websites prior to elections, to learn more about each candidate.
  2. Facilitate crowd-sourced creation and sharing of interactive ballots, linked to OFFICIAL candidate websites.
  3. Promote media literacy and web design skills for students and citizens.
