Why Interactive Ballot?

Our politics are too polarized, and many of us walk into the voting booth knowing only the names we have seen in headlines and ads. Interactive Ballot is part of Heal Our Culture, an effort to reduce political polarization and help neighbors take part as informed citizens.

The project has two goals:

  • Help voters and citizens arrive at the polls better informed. Every candidate on your ballot, from president to school board, gets the same treatment and the same kind of link.
  • Encourage candidates to use their websites and digital resources to explain their ideas, experience, background, and fitness and desirability for office. When a candidate has no website, your ballot will say so plainly, so voters can see who makes that information easy to find.

How it works

You bring your own sample ballot. An AI tool does the research and builds a page just for your ballot, with a link to each candidate's official website, or the next best source when there isn't one.

  1. Get your sample ballot as a PDF. Use the state finder just below. Your county election office posts a sample ballot for each precinct, and most states point you there through their official voter lookup.
  2. Open an AI tool and attach the PDF. We built this with Claude and recommend a Claude Pro plan, because researching every candidate and building the page takes a lot of work. Other AI tools that can read a PDF, search the web, and create files may also work.
  3. Paste the prompt below and send it. Review what you get, click the links, and print the cheat sheet to take with you.

    Sharing tips

    • Host your file anywhere that serves web pages, such as GitHub Pages, or simply share the HTML file.

    • If you share it, keep it nonpartisan, and say when the links were gathered.

    Questions

    Does this tell me who to vote for?

    No. Every candidate gets the same treatment and links. The goal is to make it easier to learn about each person, not to steer anyone.

    Where are my picks saved?

    Only in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases them, so use the print button for a copy.

    Do I have to use Claude?

    No. The prompt is plain text. Any AI tool that can read a PDF, search the web, and create a file may work, but results vary. We built these with Claude and recommend a Claude Pro plan for a job this large.

    What if a candidate has no website?

    The prompt tells the AI to say so plainly and link the next best source, such as Ballotpedia or a nonpartisan voter guide, and to label it as third-party so you know it is not the candidate's own site.

    Can I use it outside North Carolina?

    Yes. Any sample ballot PDF from any state or county should work. Local races may have fewer sources, so expect more third-party links.
